> Music is your co instructor. Sound design shapes effort, emotion, and meaning.

## Why this module exists
Track selection by taste alone produces inconsistent outcomes. Sonic design requires explicit intent: what state you are initiating, amplifying, and integrating.

## Core concepts
### Arousal valence map
Classify tracks by energy and emotional tone.

### ISO principle
Begin near current participant state and shift gradually toward target state.

### Five Channel Score
1. Percussion
2. Harmony
3. Texture
4. Vocals
5. Silence

## BPM ranges by arc phase
| Phase | BPM range | Primary function |
|---|---:|---|
| Arrival | 70 to 95 | Ground and orient |
| Warm build | 95 to 115 | Mobilize and commit |
| Escalation | 115 to 132 | Increase intensity |
| Cathartic peak | 130 to 152 | Concentrated output |
| Integration | 95 to 118 | Recover coherence |
| Landing | 60 to 90 | Downshift and consolidate |

## What the evidence actually says
Music can improve affective response and adherence for many participants. Effects depend on preference, context, culture, and task demand.

## Practicum
1. Build one sixty minute score map.
2. Annotate each track using five channels.
3. Test alternative peak tracks.
4. Add planned silence windows.

## Deliverable
Submit a Sonic Design Score with arc map, channel annotations, and contingency swaps.

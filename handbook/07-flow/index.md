> Flow cannot be forced, but it can be designed for through challenge calibration, clear goals, and precise feedback loops.

## Why this module exists
Flow is often framed as accidental magic. In practice, it is an interaction between task structure, perceived capability, and feedback clarity.

## Core concepts
- Csikszentmihalyi on challenge skill balance.
- Sawyer on group flow and collaborative emergence.
- Attention design as performance architecture.

## Eight design levers
1. Challenge and skill calibration
2. Clear goals per segment
3. Immediate feedback loops
4. Temporal structure and phrase predictability
5. Attention narrowing at peaks
6. Agency windows for co creation
7. Affective contour across arc phases
8. Integration interval for consolidation

## What the evidence actually says
Flow correlates with intrinsic motivation and persistence. Not all high intensity absorption is flow; anxiety and dissociation can mimic similar surface markers.

## Practicum
1. Diagnose one segment where flow collapses.
2. Redesign that segment with all eight levers.
3. Pilot and collect observations.
4. Revise using behavior data and participant language.

## Deliverable
Submit a Flow Engineering Brief documenting design decisions, pilot evidence, and revisions.

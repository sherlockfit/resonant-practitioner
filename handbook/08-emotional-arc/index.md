> This module introduces the original methodology contribution of this handbook: the Six Phase Cathartic Arc.

## Why this module exists
Arc design translates theory into lived sequence. Without clear arc logic, classes oscillate between monotony and chaos.

## Six Phase Cathartic Arc
| Phase | Purpose | Duration | Music | Cueing | Somatic target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arrival and Orientation | Establish safety and baseline | 6 to 10 min | Warm low arousal | Grounding invitational | Breath and contact awareness |
| Activation and Mobilization | Build heat and commitment | 8 to 12 min | Rising pulse | Specific rhythmic | Joint prep and core readiness |
| Escalation and Edge | Expand challenge | 10 to 14 min | Driving groove | Short direct options | Effort tolerance and coordination |
| Cathartic Peak | Focused expressive output | 8 to 12 min | High energy | Sparse potent containment | Whole body intensity and agency |
| Integration and Downshift | Restore coherence | 10 to 14 min | Softened texture | Interoceptive reflective | Down regulation and breath length |
| Landing and Meaning | Consolidate and close | 6 to 10 min | Spacious minimal | Quiet precise | Parasympathetic settling |

## What the evidence actually says
The arc is a design framework, not a guaranteed emotional outcome. Catharsis helps some participants and destabilizes others. Option rich cueing and clear scaling are non negotiable.

## Practicum
1. Map your current class to six phases.
2. Identify one mismatch per phase.
3. Build alternate low energy and high energy versions.
4. Pilot twice and compare outcomes.

## Deliverable
Create a Six Phase Arc Blueprint with timestamps, score choices, cue skeleton, and risk controls.

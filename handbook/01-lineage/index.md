> Lineage study is method design due diligence. Know the field before claiming originality.

## Why this module exists
Most movement systems share recurring motifs: rhythmic escalation, identity language, and social regulation through synchronized effort. Studying lineage helps you distinguish authentic innovation from repackaging.

## The Class as field study
Treat Taryn Toomey and The Class as a live case of ritualized movement pedagogy. Distinguishing features include repetition under rhythmic pressure, permission for emotional expression, and deliberate sonic arc design.

## What the evidence actually says
Social identity and belonging improve exercise adherence. Music and synchronized timing can support enjoyment and effort. Claims of universal trauma release from intense classes remain unsupported.

## Practicum
1. Observe three classes from different lineages.
2. Complete one comparison sheet per class.
3. Draft your lineage map with explicit boundaries.
4. Write a method boundary statement.

## Deliverable
Create a Lineage Dossier with matrix notes, observations, and your boundary statement.

> Emotional potency does not replace mechanical literacy. Sustainable class design depends on anatomy informed choices.

## Why this module exists
High repetition formats produce cumulative stress patterns rather than single maximal strain. Coaches must read alignment drift, breathing breakdown, and compensatory movement under fatigue.

## Core concepts
### Joint regions
- Foot and ankle: tripod contact and tibial progression.
- Knee: frontal plane control and tracking.
- Hip and pelvis: load sharing without lumbar substitution.
- Spine and thorax: mobility where needed and stiffness where needed.
- Shoulder complex: scapular rhythm and overhead tolerance.

### Fascia
Useful claim: fascia contributes to force transmission and elastic behavior. Overclaim alert: fascia memory narratives are mostly metaphorical, not established tissue memory science.

### Breath mechanics
Coach diaphragmatic excursion, rib mobility, and paced exhale. Under fatigue, people brace and breathe shallowly, reducing coordination quality.

### Gait relevance
Even studio formats affect gait through hip extension, trunk rotation, and ankle stiffness management.

## Common injury patterns
| Pattern | Typical driver | Early sign | Coaching response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anterior knee pain | Valgus under fatigue | Front knee ache | Track knee and scale depth |
| Achilles overload | Rapid plyo increase | Morning stiffness | Gradual dosage and eccentrics |
| Lumbar irritation | Hinge loss under fatigue | Post class tightness | Rebuild hinge and trunk control |
| Shoulder irritation | Overhead volume with poor rhythm | Pinch on arc | Reduce range and re align |

## What the evidence actually says
Load management and technique quality reduce risk but do not eliminate risk. No single cue guarantees safety. Progressive overload can be achieved through tempo, density, complexity, and volume without heavy external load.

## Practicum
1. Film foundational patterns.
2. Annotate compensations by region.
3. Design regression and progression ladders.
4. Build a red flag checklist.

## Deliverable
Submit a Biomechanics Coaching Sheet with cue priorities, adaptation ladder, and stop modify criteria.

> Synthesis becomes authorship in this module. You move from analysis to method construction.

## Why this module exists
Original methods are built through constraints, language systems, pilots, and revision cycles. Naming alone is not methodology.

## Lab sequence
1. Define the problem your method solves.
2. Name three to five methodology pillars.
3. Design your signature class arc.
4. Build your cue and language system.
5. Draft certification architecture.

## Certification principles
- Protect participant safety first.
- Assess both technical and relational skill.
- Include ethics and evidence literacy as required competencies.

## Workbook integration
Use the companion workbook continuously:

- [Methodology Design Lab — 60-Page Workbook](../../workbook/)

Suggested pacing is ten exercises per week across six weeks.

## Practicum
1. Complete workbook exercises 1 to 30.
2. Teach two prototype sessions.
3. Gather participant and self audit evidence.
4. Revise pillars and language accordingly.

## Deliverable
Submit a Method Prototype Dossier with problem statement, pillars, arc map, cue glossary, pilot findings, and certification outline.

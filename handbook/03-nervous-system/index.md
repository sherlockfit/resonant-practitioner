> Intensity can support integration or trigger overwhelm. Choice, pacing, and context determine the difference.

## Why this module exists
Movement spaces increasingly use therapeutic language without therapeutic infrastructure. This module builds trauma sensitive pedagogy that remains within movement educator scope.

## Core concepts
### ANS basics
Autonomic state shifts influence attention, tolerance, and social connection in class.

### Interoception
Interoception is perception of internal bodily signals. It can be trainable for many participants, with variable outcomes.

### Polyvagal theory with critique
Stephen Porges provided a widely used map for state dependent behavior. Several specific claims remain contested. Use as heuristic, not settled doctrine.

## Trauma sensitive teaching principles
1. Choice over coercion.
2. Predictability with flexible options.
3. Consent aware tactile policy.
4. Agency preserving language.
5. Window of tolerance pacing.

## Contraindications and caution flags
- Hyperventilation prone participants in breath intense peaks.
- Recent concussion or unresolved vestibular symptoms.
- Active psychiatric instability requiring clinical care.
- Histories where forced catharsis increases dissociation.

## What the evidence actually says
Exercise supports mood and stress outcomes for many people. Body based regulation practices are promising but heterogeneous. Guaranteed trauma release claims are unsupported.

## Working examples
- Panic escalation mid class: orient, downshift, extend exhale, offer seated options.
- Numbing after peak: sensory grounding, bilateral movement, simple tracking prompts.

## Practicum
1. Rewrite coercive script lines into invitational alternatives.
2. Build an upshift stabilize downshift ladder.
3. Practice a ninety second grounding protocol.
4. Draft referral boundary language.

## Deliverable
Produce a Trauma Sensitive Operations Card with principles, contraindications, interventions, and referral boundaries.

> This module turns inspiration into accountable practice. You will define what you are building, for whom, and how progress will be measured.

## Why this module exists
A self-directed graduate program fails without structure. Orientation gives you a working contract with yourself: scope, cadence, metrics, and deliverables. It is the difference between consuming ideas and building a method.

## Core concepts
### Personal mandate
Write one statement that includes population, transformation, method, and ethical boundary.

### Lineage map introduction
Before [Module 1](../01-lineage/), sketch direct influences, adjacent influences, and explicit departures.

### Progress measurement
Track behavior and outcomes together.

| Domain | Weekly indicator | Monthly indicator |
|---|---|---|
| Practice | Deliberate sessions completed | Arc coherence improvement |
| Teaching | Cues tested in class | Return participation trend |
| Regulation | Daily readiness log | Fewer overreach episodes |
| Originality | New drills created | Signature patterns recognized |

### Study cadence
A strong weekly cadence is six to eight hours distributed across reading, physical practicum, score design, rehearsal, and deliverable production.

### Deliverable format
Every deliverable includes claim, evidence tier, application, risk, and next revision.

## What the evidence actually says
Goal setting and implementation intentions improve completion likelihood. Reflective practice works best when linked to observable behavior. Self report alone is noisy, so pair it with measurable actions.

## Working examples
- Recovery focused mandate for overloaded professionals.
- Performance focused mandate for dancers and performers.

## Practicum
1. Draft three mandate versions.
2. Build a one page lineage map draft.
3. Define four measurable outcomes.
4. Calendar the next twelve weeks.
5. Create your portfolio folder structure.

## Deliverable
Submit a Personal Mandate Packet with mandate statement, lineage draft, metrics dashboard, and weekly cadence.

This module is the contract you sign with yourself before the program begins. Skip it and the rest of the modules become entertainment. Do it well and every later decision — what to read, what to drill, what to ship — gets faster and clearer because you'll know what you're optimizing for.

> Block ninety minutes this week. Phone in another room. Notebook or document open. Do the four exercises in order. By the end of the week you'll have a single document — your **Orientation Packet** — that the rest of the program is built on top of.

---

## Why this module exists

You are not enrolling in a school. You are running a school of one, and you are both faculty and student. That arrangement only works if you write down what you're trying to become and how you'll know you've moved.

Three things go wrong without an orientation:

1. **Drift.** You read interesting things, take interesting classes, and a year later you can't point to anything that has changed about your teaching.
2. **Borrowed mandate.** You end up training to become a slightly different version of someone you admire instead of training to become a more fully realized version of yourself.
3. **No exit criteria.** You can't tell whether you're done with a module, a season, or the whole program, so you stay vague forever.

The four exercises below — Mandate, Lineage Map, Outcome Contract, and Cadence — are how you avoid all three.

---

## Exercise 1 — Personal Mandate (20 min)

Write one paragraph (no more than 150 words) that finishes this sentence in your own words:

> *"I am building this practice so that ____ , and I will know I'm doing it well when ____ ."*

A strong mandate has four parts. Make sure yours covers all four:

- **Population.** Who specifically benefits from your work? "Everyone" is not an answer.
- **Transformation.** What is concretely different in their body, week, or life because of what you do?
- **Method signature.** What is *your* angle — what would feel wrong about doing this any other way?
- **Boundary.** What you are *not* doing. (You are not a therapist. You are not a physician. Name the line out loud.)

Draft three versions. Keep the one that makes you slightly uncomfortable because it's more specific than feels safe.

---

## Exercise 2 — Lineage Map (25 min)

Sketch a one-page map — literally on paper or in a document — with three concentric rings:

1. **Direct teachers.** People whose classes you have actually taken. Write their names and one phrase for what you took from each.
2. **Influences at a distance.** Writers, methodologies, traditions you've studied but not trained inside. Same treatment — one phrase each.
3. **Departures.** What you are deliberately *not* doing that adjacent practitioners do. This is where your originality lives.

You will return to this map in [Module 1](../01-lineage/) and refine it into a full comparative matrix. For now, the job is to get it out of your head onto a page.

---

## Exercise 3 — 12-Month Outcome Contract (25 min)

A contract with yourself, dated and signed. Copy this template, fill in every blank, and put it somewhere you will actually see it.

```
OUTCOME CONTRACT — [your name]
Start: ____    90-day review: ____    Recommit: ____

By [date 12 months from today], the following will be true:

TEACHING
- I will have taught at least ___ classes
- My signature class arc will be documented at version ≥ 1.0
- I will have ___ regular students who return month over month

CRAFT
- I will have completed Modules 0–___ of this program
- My personal cue library will contain at least ___ cues I use weekly
- I will have a music scoring system I can use to design any class in 30 min

BODY
- My own movement practice will run at ___ sessions per week, minimum
- I will not have re-aggravated [your known injury patterns]

VOICE
- I will have published ___ pieces of public writing or teaching
- I will be able to give a 90-second answer to "what do you teach?"
  without using anyone else's brand language

Signed: ______________
```

The numbers matter less than the act of writing them down. Pick numbers that would feel like genuine progress — not impossible, not safe.

---

## Exercise 4 — Study Cadence (20 min)

Decide *now*, before motivation gets a vote:

- **Weekly study block.** Pick a recurring day and time. Two to four hours, protected. Put it in your calendar before you close this page.
- **Module pace.** Default is one module every one to two weeks. Faster is fine if quality holds. Slower is fine as long as you're shipping deliverables.
- **Review ritual.** Once a month, re-read your mandate and outcome contract. Edit if your understanding has genuinely changed. Don't edit because you're tired.

---

## Deliverable

By the end of this week, produce a single document — call it `orientation-packet.md` (paper is also fine) — containing:

1. Your final **Personal Mandate**
2. Your **Lineage Map**
3. Your signed **12-Month Outcome Contract**
4. Your **weekly study block** on the calendar (a screenshot counts)

When that document exists, Module 0 is complete and you can move to Module 1.

## Default sixty minute score
| Phase | Minutes | BPM range |
|---|---:|---:|
| Arrival | 8 | 70 to 95 |
| Activation | 10 | 95 to 118 |
| Escalation | 12 | 115 to 132 |
| Peak | 10 | 130 to 152 |
| Integration | 12 | 95 to 118 |
| Landing | 8 | 60 to 90 |

## Transition library
- Keep your breath and change your shape.
- Reset your stance and keep your intention.
- Option to stay with base or progress with precision.
- One beat of stillness, then continue.
- Downshift with longer exhale before next block.

## Practicum
1. Build one class using this template.
2. Prepare three alternate peak blocks.
3. Rehearse transition language out loud.

## Deliverable
Compile a Toolkit Runbook with customized template, default score, and thirty preferred transitions.

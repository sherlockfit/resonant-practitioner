> Class design succeeds only when teaching transmission is clear, humane, and adaptive.

## Why this module exists
Instruction quality determines whether participants feel agency, confusion, or coercion. This module integrates cue craft with coaching science.

## Cueing modes
- Verbal: concise and actionable.
- Tactile: consent based and purpose specific.
- Visual: demonstration and directional signaling.
- Kinesthetic: sensation based action prompts.

## Coaching psychology
Autonomy support, competence scaffolding, and relational safety improve adherence. Shame based motivation may produce short spikes and long term disengagement.

## Behavior change models
### Transtheoretical model
Match asks to readiness stage.

### COM B
| Domain | Teaching move |
|---|---|
| Capability | Layered regressions and progressions |
| Opportunity | Inclusive social environment |
| Motivation | Meaningful framing and visible progress |

## Voice and presence
Use pacing, silence, and tonal steadiness to regulate the room. Authority comes from clarity, not volume.

## Practicum
1. Transcribe one teaching segment.
2. Tag each cue by function and mode.
3. Rewrite twenty weak cues.
4. Rehearse pacing against music transitions.

## Deliverable
Submit a Teaching Method Packet with cue audit, rewritten script, and COM B adaptation plan.

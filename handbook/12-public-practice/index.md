> Influence can responsibly scale useful practice when voice, method, and ethics stay aligned.

## Why this module exists
Some practitioners master craft but never communicate publicly. Others scale rapidly and lose rigor. This module trains durable public practice.

## Case studies
### Jane Fonda

### Jack LaLanne
Consistency and public demonstration as trust engine.

### Richard Simmons

### Tracy Anderson

### Taryn Toomey
Ritualized atmosphere and emotionally explicit class framing.

### Kayla Itsines
Platform native scaling through clear programming and tracking.

## Iconic voice playbook
1. State one clear claim.
2. Define audience and non audience.
3. Build repeatable narrative spine.
4. Publish with sustainable cadence.
5. Maintain evidence honesty under growth pressure.
6. Keep direct contact with practitioners.

## Practicum
1. Draft one sentence public claim.
2. Write origin stories at thirty second, three minute, and ten minute lengths.
3. Publish one evidence labeled teaching piece.
4. Collect and analyze structured feedback.

## Deliverable
Create an Iconic Voice Packet with claim, story set, ninety day publishing plan, and ethics safeguards.

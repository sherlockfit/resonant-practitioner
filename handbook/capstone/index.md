> The capstone proves your methodology in practice: design quality, teaching quality, safety, and reflective revision.

## Project requirement
Design and teach an original sixty minute class that explicitly synthesizes Modules 1 through 12.

## Required submission components
1. Design packet with target practitioner, arc map, score, and adaptations.
2. Teaching evidence with recording and cue transcript excerpts.
3. Assessment evidence with participant feedback and self audit.
4. Revision memo documenting iteration decisions.

## Rubric
| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| Design coherence | 20 |
| Safety and scope | 20 |
| Cue quality | 15 |
| Sonic and rhythmic design | 15 |
| Evidence literacy | 15 |
| Originality and voice | 15 |

## Practicum
1. Draft design packet.
2. Teach pilot one.
3. Revise and teach pilot two.
4. Finalize dossier.

## Deliverable
Submit a complete Capstone Dossier including all required components and revision rationale.

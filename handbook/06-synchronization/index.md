> Classes create temporary social bodies. Your responsibility is to design cohesion without coercion.

## Why this module exists
Group intensity can produce belonging, motivation, and meaning. It can also produce conformity pressure. You need sociological and relational design literacy.

## Core concepts
### Durkheim
Collective effervescence describes heightened social energy generated through shared ritual action.

### Co regulation
Participants regulate through timing, gaze, breath, and proximity.

### Mirror systems with restraint
Observational coupling supports learning, but mirror neuron explanations are often oversimplified.

### Ritual studies
Entry, threshold, peak, return: these phases structure both behavior and meaning.

## Safety and inclusion design
- Keep participation opt in.
- Provide non performative options.
- Use language that invites rather than commands identity.
- Build de intensification cues for over arousal.

## What the evidence actually says
Synchrony can improve cohesion and prosocial behavior. Effects are context dependent and can exclude out groups when identity framing becomes rigid.

## Practicum
1. Design a ten minute synchrony block with three option levels.
2. Pilot and audit inclusion signals.
3. Add one agency interrupt moment.
4. Reflect on synchronization versus conformity.

## Deliverable
Produce a Group Synchrony Protocol with structure, consent language, inclusion adaptations, and recovery actions.

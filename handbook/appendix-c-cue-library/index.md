> Use this library as modular language material. Select cues by class phase, participant profile, and objective.

## How to use this appendix
- Choose three to five anchor cues for each phase of your class.
- Keep cues short during high exertion and more descriptive during preparation or integration.
- Tag your preferred cues by phase using [Module 8](../08-emotional-arc/) and [Module 9](../09-teaching/).
- Revise wording when cues create confusion, shame, or avoidable risk.

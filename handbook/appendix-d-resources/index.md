> Read across disciplines. The strongest methods integrate physiology, psychology, anthropology, and culture.

## Annotated reading by module
### Orientation and lineage
- Taryn Toomey interviews and field profiles for language and ritual framing.
- Bourdieu selected chapters for taste and class signaling in movement culture.

### Anatomy and nervous system
- Calais Germain, Anatomy of Movement.
- Stuart McGill, Back Mechanic.
- Damasio, The Feeling of What Happens.
- Van der Kolk, The Body Keeps the Score with critical reading posture.
- Porges, The Polyvagal Theory with critique pairing.

### Rhythm, music, synchrony, flow
- Levitin, This Is Your Brain on Music.
- Patel, Music, Language, and the Brain.
- Durkheim, The Elementary Forms of Religious Life.
- Turner, The Ritual Process.
- Csikszentmihalyi, Flow.
- Sawyer, Group Genius.

### Design, method, public practice
- Wiggins and McTighe, Understanding by Design.
- Schmalzl et al. contemplative movement review literature.
- Csordas, somatic modes of attention essays.

## Podcasts
- Song Exploder for sonic literacy.
- On Being episodes on ritual and meaning.
- Selected behavior science interviews with claim verification discipline.

## Films and documentaries
- Jane Fonda in Five Acts.
- Dance rehearsal documentaries emphasizing process and pedagogy.

## In person trainings
- Functional anatomy intensives.
- Rhythm and percussion workshops.
- Voice coaching for instructors.
- Trauma informed education training within appropriate scope.

## Practicum
1. Build a twelve month reading plan linked to module sequence.
2. Pair each core text with one critique or counterpoint.
3. Update your claim log monthly.
